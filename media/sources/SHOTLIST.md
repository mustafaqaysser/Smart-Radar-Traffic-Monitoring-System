# Zill — photography & video shot list

Brand: **Zill · ظل** — an Arabian Peninsula courtyard restaurant (Jeddah & Riyadh) organised by the hours of the day.
Concept line: "Every hour has its shade." Art direction: hard, low, warm sunlight; long crisp shadows; warm highlights
and cool shade; plaster, coral stone, clay, palm wood, linen, stoneware, brass. Night frames lit by warm lamps
against deep blue ambient.

## Selection rules (reject anything that breaks one)

- Free commercial licence only: Unsplash free images (`images.unsplash.com/photo-…`, never `plus.unsplash.com` /
  `premium_photo`), or Wikimedia Commons / Openverse under CC0, Public Domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA.
- Long edge ≥ 2000 px, sharp where it matters, no heavy HDR, no obvious AI artefacts, no collages.
- No text, logos, brand labels, watermarks, menus, price tags or signage in frame.
- No alcohol (wine/beer glasses, bottles), no pork, no cigarettes, no phones/laptops.
- No identifiable faces. People only as hands, backs, silhouettes or cropped bodies.
- Warm, natural, directional light preferred; reject cold blue casts, neon, flat flash, fast-food styling,
  plastic or disposable tableware, stainless diner look.
- Food: 45° or top-down on stone/linen/wood/stoneware; room to crop to **4:5 portrait** (the menu hover window is tall).
- Consistency beats novelty: every frame must grade into one warm, earthy look.
